Our Philosophy

At Hars, we believe a fragrance is more than a scent — it is an emotion crystallised in time. Each bottle is a dialogue between the perfumer's vision and the wearer's soul.

We source only the finest ingredients — hand-harvested Bulgarian rose, aged Indian oud, Madagascan vanilla — and compose with slow, deliberate artistry from our atelier in Pune.
